Fjord — sun over water

One circle cut by a level horizon: the sun above, its reflection below as two bands of water, the second thinner. Every state below is drawn from the same construction and the studio's three colours.

Logo

The mark, the wordmark with its j dotted in water, the lockup, and the studio endorsement.

App icon

macOS asks for default, dark and a single-tint version (tinted and clear); the tint keeps the shape, so the water is told apart by its cuts alone.

Sizes

One mark at every size: macOS scales the 1024 px icon down for Finder and Spotlight, and Fjord has no menu bar item, so there is no separate small drawing.

Loading

In one colour, as everywhere in the app: the parts fade out one by one from the top, the sun first, then come back in the same order.

States

The mark carries no status, and no colour for it: at rest, or working. Needs you and failed stay in the app's own interface, where they already are.
